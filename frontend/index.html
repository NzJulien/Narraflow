Turn your voice into an illustrated story.

Just tell a story out loud. NarraFlow listens, understands who and where and what happens, and paints each scene as you go. Ask it to change anything, by voice, and watch the pictures follow.

How it works

The voice agent doesn't just talk. It acts on a structured story through tool calls.

  • Real conversation. Turn detection tuned for storytellers who pause, and instant barge-in when you talk over the agent.
  • Tools, not just talk. Create, add and edit scenes, change a character everywhere, restyle the whole story, reorder or remove scenes (with a spoken confirmation), play and export.
  • Consistent characters. Each character's look is stored once and injected into every illustration prompt.
  • Never blocks the conversation. Painting runs in the background; the agent keeps talking while scenes appear.
  • Secure by design. Server-issued temporary tokens, rate-limited, no secrets in the browser.

Who it's for

Anyone who tells stories better than they draw them.

NarraFlow began as a text-to-video multi-agent demo and has been rebuilt as a voice-first illustrated storyteller for the AssemblyAI Voice Agent Hackathon 2026. Its original pipeline is still available as the original demo.
Illustrations use the configured image provider. Without an image API key, a simple built-in storybook illustrator is used so the whole flow still works. Exported videos are silent with burned-in captions; spoken narration plays in the app.
